If you're a researcher, it’s likely you have an [ORCID iD](https://orcid.org/). Your ORCID iD is your unique persistent identifier (PID) and provides a reliable link between you and your research activities. [DataCite DOIs](https://support.datacite.org/docs/doi-basics) (Digital Object Identifiers) provide a unique identifier for your research outputs. DOIs are always accompanied by metadata, which allows connections to be made between the research shared with a DOI and other things shared with a PID - like organizations with a [ROR ID](https://ror.org/) and researchers with an ORCID iD.

We’ve worked with ORCID and their APIs to make it easy for you to make sure all of your research is associated with your ORCID record. To help you get started, we’ve created a  one-page guide:

[Elevate Your Visibility and Impact: Connect Your ORCID Record to Your Research with DataCite DOIs](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17940667)
