## Technical support

If you have questions about DataCite, our services, need a test account, or any other general inquiry, please contact us at [support@datacite.org](mailto:support@datacite.org).

> 👍 
> 
> If your organization is part of a Consortium, please contact your Consortium Lead first.

Our support team will be happy to assist you. Please consider the following before you submit a technical support request:

- Our [support pages](https://support.datacite.org/) provide information and guides about using all of our products including the [REST API](doc:api).
- You can view and subscribe to [status alerts](http://status.datacite.org/) for our services on our status page.

> 📘 Tips for submitting a technical support request
> 
> Clear and detailed support requests help us help you! Once you are ready to submit a support request, please do the following:
> 
> - Provide a clear and detailed description with as much information as possible, including your account ID and the DOI name (if relevant).
> - Provide steps to reproduce the issue, if applicable.
> 
> If possible, you can also include:
> 
> - Crash logs (for crashes/bugs)
> - Screenshots (showing error messages)
> - Sample project or source code
> - Console output (containing errors or exceptions)

### Usersnap

On the bottom right of every DataCite Fabrica and Commons page you will see the “Feedback” button. This useful tool allows you to submit a screenshot directly to our support team.

### Bug reports

Encounter a bug or other system issue? Contact [support@datacite.org](mailto:support@datacite.org) for prompt support. We will work with you to assess the issue and provide solutions and workarounds.

You can view the status of existing bugs on our [GitHub page](http://github.com/datacite).

## Feature suggestions

If you have suggestions for new DataCite features or improvements to existing features, please submit your ideas via the [DataCite Roadmap](https://datacite.org/roadmap.html).

### Metadata Schema suggestions

We are interested in your suggestions to improve the DataCite Metadata Schema. Learn more about [how to contribute](https://schema.datacite.org/contribute.html).

## Membership enquiries

For questions about membership or invoicing, please contact us at[info@datacite.org](mailto:info@datacite.org).
