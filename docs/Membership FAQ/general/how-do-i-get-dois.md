DataCite does not provide individual DOIs but enables research organizations that join DataCite as members directly or through a consortium to provide DOIs. If your organization is already a [DataCite member](https://datacite.org/members.html) you can register DOIs for your research outputs with the DOI services of your organization. Alternatively, please see the [re3data repository registry for a repository](https://www.re3data.org/) that offers DOI services and that fits your scope.
