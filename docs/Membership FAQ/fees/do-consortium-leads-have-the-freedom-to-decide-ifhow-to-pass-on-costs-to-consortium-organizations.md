Yes, consortium leads can decide to pay the membership fee themselves or split the membership fee amongst the consortium organizations. In addition, there are examples of consortium leads that add a management fee on top of the service fees that DataCite charges consortium organizations. DataCite thinks it’s important that consortia can work in a way that suits them and leaves it up to the consortia to decide on fees within the consortium.
