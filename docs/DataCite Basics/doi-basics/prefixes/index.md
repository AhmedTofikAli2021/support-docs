A DOI prefix always starts with '10.' and continues with a number (e.g. '10.1234' or '10.20865'). The DOI prefix is used as a namespace so that DOIs are globally unique. DOI names are provided by DataCite and other DOI Registration Agencies (RAs). This is coordinated by the [International DOI Foundation (IDF)](https://www.doi.org/).

• There are [11 DOI Registration Agencies](https://www.doi.org/registration_agencies.html), most of which assign DOI names to scholarly content. [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/) is the largest DOI Registration Agency.

• [CNRI](https://www.cnri.reston.va.us/) (Corporation for National Research Initiatives) manage the [handle system](http://handle.net/) and provide technical and operational support for the DOI system as a contractor. They assign a pool of prefixes to DataCite which are imported into Fabrica and distributed to DataCite Members.
