If you have questions about DataCite, our services, membership, need a demo account, or any other general inquiry, please contact [support@datacite.org](mailto:support@datacite.org) directly. We will get back to you promptly.

Please note at this time our support is provided in English.

DataCite maintains an open [All Users mailing list](https://groups.google.com/a/datacite.org/forum/#!forum/allusers). Please join us for the latest news, webinars, conferences and useful information for DataCite partners, integrators, users, and supporters.

DataCite maintains a closed **All Members** list dedicated to sharing the latest information about DataCite for our members.  If you are a member organization, please join the closed All Members list.  Contact [support@datacite.org](mailto:support@datacite.org) to join the list or update your subscription information.
