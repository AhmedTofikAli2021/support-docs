An organization's design represents its identity. The purpose of [DataCite Design Manual](https://datacite.org/assets/DataCite_Design_manual-version_1_0.pdf) is to provide guidelines for the use of DataCite design elements such as our logo, colors, and fonts to highlight our identity and make it recognizable to our community at all times.

An organizational design is essential for a consistent visual experience of DataCite’s services and products. It is also important because it represents DataCite’s identity, mission, and values. We therefore encourage you to follow the guidelines of this design manual.

Please contact us at info@datacite.org if you have questions about how to apply these guidelines.
