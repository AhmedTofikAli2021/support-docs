DataCite Members, Consortium Organizations and registered Service Providers may use DataCite’s name, logo, and the relevant community badge to announce their status and their activities. For any other use of the materials available in the [DataCite design manual](https://datacite.org/assets/DataCite_Design_manual-version_1_0.pdf), please contact [info@datacite.org](mailto:info@datacite.org).
