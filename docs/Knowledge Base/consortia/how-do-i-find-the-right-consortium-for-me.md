If you would like to join DataCite as part of a Consortium we can help you find the right Consortium for your organization. Please contact us at [support@datacite.org](mailto:support@datacite.org) to discuss your options.

You can see the list of existing [DataCite Consortia on the DataCite website](https://datacite.org/members/).
