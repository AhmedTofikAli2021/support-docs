You can access DataCite Fabrica with [different types of accounts](doc:datacite-account-types). Each account has different permissions. If you log into DataCite Fabrica with a Member or Consortium Organization account (e.g. OSDV), you have permission to [update contacts](doc:fabrica-contacts) and create Repositories. However, you won’t be able to create DOIs. 

> 📘 Create a Repository Account
> 
> You need a Repository account to create and manage DOIs. You can [create a Repository](doc:fabrica-repositories#create-a-repository) with your Direct Member or Consortium Organization account.

To create **DOIs and metadata**, you must use your Repository account e.g. OSDV.CLTNJL (this also applies to the DataCite [REST API](doc:api) and [MDS API](doc:mds-api-guide)).

When you are logged in with a Repository account you will see:

- The Create DOI button
- The role "Repository" on the Info tab
- The Repository account ID to the top right of the Fabrica page and in the Settings tab
