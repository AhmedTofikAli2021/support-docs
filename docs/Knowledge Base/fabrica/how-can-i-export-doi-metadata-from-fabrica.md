When you log into [Fabrica](https://doi.datacite.org/) with your Consortium lead/Consortium Organization/Member or Repository account you can now export all of the DOI metadata from the DOIs tab on the dashboard. 

When you navigate to the DOIs tab, the “Export DOI Metadata” button appears on the top left. Click here to export all of the basic metadata of the DOIs in the list into a CSV file.

Learn more about exporting [metadata from Fabrica](doc:export-doi-metadata).
