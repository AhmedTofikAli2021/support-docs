It is mandatory to include the required contacts in your organization’s Fabrica account. If you are logged in with a Member, Consortium or Consortium Organization account and you see the following message, this means you need to add the required contact information:

“New repositories can't be created because you have not provided the required billing, service and voting contacts for your organization. Please add at least one contact."

This is done in two steps:

1. Creating contacts in the “Contacts'' tab.
2. Assigning a role in the “Settings'' tab.

Check the guide for information about how to create and manage [Contacts in DataCite Fabrica](doc:fabrica-contacts).
