It is mandatory to include the required contacts in your organization’s Fabrica account. If you are logged in with a Member, Consortium or Consortium Organization account and you see the message following message:

“New repositories can't be created because you have not provided the required billing, service and voting contacts for your organization. Please add at least one contact”.

This means you need to add the required contact information. This is done in two steps 1.) creating contacts in the “Contacts'' tab, and then 2.) assigning a role in the “Settings'' tab. Check the guide for information about how to [create and manage contacts in Fabrica](doc:add-contacts).
