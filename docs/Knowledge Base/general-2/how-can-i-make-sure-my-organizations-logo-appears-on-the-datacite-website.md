The organizational information that appears on the Members page of the [DataCite website](https://datacite.org/members/) for Direct Members and Consortia is synced directly from DataCite Fabrica. You can update all of the information about your organization, including the logo, in the Settings tab of the DataCite Member or Consortium Lead account dashboard. You may upload a horizontal logo in either png or jpeg format. 

For more information, see [Settings in DataCite Fabrica](doc:fabrica-settings).
