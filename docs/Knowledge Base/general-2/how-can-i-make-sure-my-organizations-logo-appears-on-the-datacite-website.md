The organizational information that appears on the Members page of the [DataCite website](https://datacite.org/members/) for Direct Members and Consortia is synced directly from DataCite Fabrica. You can update all of the information about your organization, including the logo, in the settings tab of the DataCite Member or Consortium Lead account dashboard. You just need to log in to Fabrica and navigate to the “Settings tab” and click “Update Organization”. You can upload a horizontal logo in either png or jpeg format.

Update settings and include the logo for [Direct Members accounts](doc:doc:fabrica-update-member) or [Consortium Lead accounts](doc:update-organization-settings-consortium-lead).
