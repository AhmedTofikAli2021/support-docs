There are two ways that works with a DataCite DOI can be linked to your ORCID record: 

[ORCID auto-update](https://support.datacite.org/docs/datacite-and-orcid#adding-a-work-to-your-orcid-record)  
When you enable auto-update, DOI records are automatically added to your ORCID record when your ORCID iD is included in the creators metadata of a created or updated DataCite DOI. Items within an ORCID record that have been asserted by a member integrator (e.g. DataCite) [will show the green check mark icon next to the source](https://support.orcid.org/hc/en-us/articles/17697191558423-Why-do-some-items-in-my-record-have-a-green-check-mark-icon).

[ORCID Search & Link ](https://support.datacite.org/docs/datacite-and-orcid#1-orcid-search--link-wizard)  
ORCID Search & Link functionality allows a researcher to manually add a DOI record to their ORCID record using [DataCite Commons](https://commons.datacite.org/). In this case, the researcher is the assertion source rather than DataCite. According to the [ORCID source classifications](https://support.orcid.org/hc/en-us/articles/360022948733-Where-can-I-see-the-source-of-information-on-my-record), these records are not given the green check mark and are instead attributed to the researcher.

Read more about what a [source is in the ORCID documentation](https://info.orcid.org/ufaqs/what-does-source-mean-in-an-orcid-record/) and also find out more about the [changes to the ORCID/DataCite integration](https://github.com/datacite/datacite-suggestions/discussions/187).
