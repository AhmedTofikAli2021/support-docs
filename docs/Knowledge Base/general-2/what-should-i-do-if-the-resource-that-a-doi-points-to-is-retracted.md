A DOI resolves to a [landing page](doc:landing-pages) where the resource is made available, usually by a repository. DOIs are persistent identifiers and are intended to be a permanent means of identifying a resource. For this reason, the DOI name and metadata will continue to exist even after the underlying content has been removed. In the case a of retraction follow these steps:

  * Change the state of the DOI to "registered" state. This archives the DOI and means it is no longer indexed anywhere. Learn more about [DOI states](doc:doi-states).
  
  * Update the URL of the erroneous DOI to a tombstone page. This can be your organization's own tombstone page or a generic one. Learn more about [tombstone pages](doc:tombstone-pages).
