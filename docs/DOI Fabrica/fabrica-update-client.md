Clients can update their settings, and Providers can update the settings of their respective Clients. 

To modify the Client settings, go to the Settings tab in the Client dashboard. Providers will first need to select the Client from their list of Clients in order to view the appropriate Client dashboard. Click the *Edit Client* button in the upper right.

Enter any changes in the appropriate fields and click *Save* at the bottom of the page. **Note:** The Client ID is fixed at time of Client creation and cannot be changed. 

For detailed descriptions of the fields, see [Field Descriptions for Client Settings](doc:fields-client-settings).
