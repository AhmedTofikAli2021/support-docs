DataCite DOI Fabrica is a one-stop service to create and manage DOIs for your organization. Fabrica is available only to DataCite Providers and Clients. 

**Providers** use Fabrica to administer Client accounts and activities related to DOI registration. **Clients** use Fabrica to create and manage DOIs and their associated metadata. [Learn more about the difference between Providers and Clients.](doc:members-providers-and-clients)
