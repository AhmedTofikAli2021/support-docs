DataCite Fabrica is a one-stop service for DataCite users to manage their account information and DOI registration.

## Account types and permissions

All [DataCite account types](doc:datacite-account-types) can access DataCite Fabrica and have different permissions:

- **Direct Members and Consortium Organizations** use DataCite Fabrica to administer Repositories and prefixes.
- **Consortium Leads** use DataCite Fabrica to create and manage their Consortium Organizations.
- **Repositories** use DataCite Fabrica to create and manage DOIs and associated metadata.

## Locate your account credentials

Your DataCite account credentials are used to access Fabrica and consist of an Account ID and password.

- **Direct Member** and **Consortium Lead** account credentials are provided during the DataCite onboarding process. If you do not yet have account credentials, please [contact us](mailto:support@datacite.org).
- **Consortium Organization** account credentials are provided by the Consortium Lead. If your organization is part of a consortium, please contact your Consortium Lead for account credentials.
- **Repository** account credentials are provided by the Direct Member or Consortium Organization managing the Repository account. If you manage a Repository, please contact your organization for account credentials.

If you do not have an account and are interested in joining DataCite, please review the information on our [website](https://datacite.org/become.html). 

If you are unsure whether your organization may already be a Direct Member or part of a consortium, please see the full list [here](https://datacite.org/members.html).

## Set your account password

When you first set up your account, you will need to create a password. The system email contact associated with the account will receive an automated email with a prompt to set a password for the account. You can [reset your password](doc:reset-fabrica-password) at any time.
