It is possible for a Consortium to delete Consortium Organizations. If the Consortium Organization has Repositories and Prefixes, these must first be deleted.

Go to the Consortium Organizations tab of the Consortium dashboard and click on the name of the Consortium Organization you wish to delete. 

You will be taken to the Consortium Organization dashboard. Go to the Settings tab and click the *Delete* button on the left.
