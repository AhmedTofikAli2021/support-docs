You can manage your DOIs through DataCite Fabrica. This includes:

- **Create and Update DOIs**: Repository accounts can create and update DOIs with the [DOI Form](doc:fabrica-doi-form) and [File Upload](doc:fabrica-doi-file-upload) tools. Repositories can also [delete Draft records](doc:fabrica-delete-doi).
- **Transfer DOIs between Repositories**: Direct Members, Consortium Leads, and Consortium Organizations can [transfer DOIs between the Repositories they manage](doc:fabrica-transfer-dois).
- **Export DOI Metadata**: All account types can search and [export DOI metadata](doc:export-doi-metadata).
