Once you have sent the required information to [support@datacite.org(mailto:support@datacite.org), DataCite, together with you, will schedule a time and date for the transfer of the prefix(es) and DOIs will be set. Make sure that all relevant parties are aware and have agreed to the transfer and copy all parties on the message to DataCite.
