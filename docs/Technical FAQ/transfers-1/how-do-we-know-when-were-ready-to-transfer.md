If you are transitioning to [Direct Membership](doc:which-categories-of-membership-are-there-1) you will be contacted by DataCite to confirm that your new membership is active. You will then be invited to an on-boarding session and will receive your production credentials. Once this is step is complete, you are ready to transfer. 

If you are transitioning as [Consortium Organization](which-categories-of-membership-are-there-1), you will be contacted by your Consortium Lead to confirm that your new repository environment is set up.

In both cases, a new repository environment will need to be created for the prefix(es) and DOIs. It is important to note that prefixes cannot be shared between repositories.
