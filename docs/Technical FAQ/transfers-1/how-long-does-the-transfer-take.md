We schedule one hour of downtime. Depending on the number of DOIs to be transferred this may take more or less time. During this time, all services will be working (Resolutions, DOI minting, Search, Link checking, Stats, Metrics) but you cannot register DOIs or modify existing DOIs in the old repository environment.
