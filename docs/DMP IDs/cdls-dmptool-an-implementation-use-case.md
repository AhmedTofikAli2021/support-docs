CDL’s DMPTool is a platform for DMP creation and guidance with the goal of facilitating the creation of next-generation, machine-actionable data management plans (DMPs). <https://dmptool.org>

The DMPTool team was interested in registering persistent identifiers for data management plans so that the DMP could be referenced in other systems during the course of the research project. A DMP ID would allow connections to be made to research outputs, the researcher and institutional identifiers.
