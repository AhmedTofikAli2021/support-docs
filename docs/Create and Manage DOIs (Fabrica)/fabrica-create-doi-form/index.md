Navigate to the DOIs tab in the Client dashboard. Click the orange *Create DOI (Form)* button in the upper right of the screen.

Enter the DOI, the state, and the metadata. For detailed explanations of each field, see [Field Descriptions for Form](doc:fields-form)

Once you've filled in the appropriate fields, click the *Create DOI* button at the bottom of the page. Your DOI will be saved in Fabrica, and the record page for the DOI will be displayed.
