It is possible for a Provider to delete one of their Clients. 

If the Client has registered DOIs, the Provider must first [transfer the DOIs](doc:transfer-dois-between-clients) to another Client managed by the same Provider.

After any remaining DOIs have been transferred, go to the Clients tab of the Provider dashboard and click on the name of the Client you wish to delete.

The next screen will warn you that this action cannot be undone. You must type the Client ID in the box to confirm the deletion. Then click the *Delete* button.

You will not be able to delete a Client that still has associated DOIs. When you click on *Delete Client*, you will instead see the screen below, asking you to first transfer all DOIs to another client.

Click on *Transfer DOIs* to initiate the DOI transfer, described in [Transfer DOIs Between Clients](doc:transfer-dois-between-clients).
