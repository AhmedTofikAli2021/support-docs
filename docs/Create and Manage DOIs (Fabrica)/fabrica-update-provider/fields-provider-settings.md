The following are descriptions of each field in the Provider Settings. 
[block:api-header]
{
  "title": "Organization Information"
}
[/block]
## Provider ID

The Provider ID is a unique identifier for each Provider. It must contain uppercase letters or numbers and should be a short meaningful string representing the Provider. 

**Note:** The Provider ID is established during onboarding and can't be modified.

## Organization Identifier

*This field is optional.* The [ROR ID](https://www.ror.org) for  your organization. Use the lookup to search the ROR registry for your organization's ROR ID. 

## Provider Name

Full name of the Provider. This is typically the name of the organization, written as the organization wants it displayed. For example, "The British Library". The contents of this field will appear on the DataCite members page. 

## Website

The URL of the Provider's primary website. The contents of this field will appear on the DataCite members page. 

## Twitter
*This field is optional.* The Twitter handle for the Provider. If this information is provided, DataCite will follow you on Twitter. 

## Country
*This field is optional.* The country where the Provider is physically located. The contents of this field will appear on the DataCite members page. 

## Organization Type
*This field is optional.* Please select the type of organization that best describes the Provider. The contents of this field will appear on the DataCite members page. 

## Focus Area
*This field is optional.* Please select the field of research that best describes the primary focus of the materials described by the Provider's DOIs. The contents of this field will appear on the DataCite members page. 

## Description
*This field is optional.* A description of the Provider.

## General Contact
The primary email address that you would like DataCite to use to communicate with you, including sending password reset requests. If you have a generic shared email address (such as info@datacite.org or similar), you may use that here. 

## Service Contact
*These fields are optional.* The service contact is the primary person responsible for the Provider from an account management perspective. This person is considered to be the primary contact for any necessary DataCite or system communications. Please provide this person's name and email address.

## Technical Contact
*These fields are optional.* The technical contact is the person responsible for maintaining any custom integrations with DataCite services the Provider may have built, or otherwise the person most suited for discussing troubleshooting with DataCite technical staff. Please provide this person's name and email address.

## Voting Contact
*These fields are optional.* The voting contact is the person responsible for casting the Provider's vote in the DataCite General Assembly. Please provide this person's name and email address.

## Billing Organization
The name of the organization as it should appear on invoices. 

## Billing Department
The specific department at your organization that is responsible for payment.

## Billing Contact
The primary person responsible for handling payment to DataCite. Please provide this person's name and email address. 

## Secondary Billing Contact
A secondary person responsible for handing payment to DataCite, to be used as a back up. Please provide this person's name and email address. 

## Billing Street
The street and number of your organization's mailing address, as it should appear on invoices. 

## Billing City
The city in your organization's mailing address, as it should appear on invoices. 

## Billing State/Province
The state or province in your organization's mailing address (if applicable), as it should appear on invoices. 

## Billing Zip/PostCode
The zip code or postal code in your organization's mailing address, as it should appear on invoices. 

## Bill Country
The country in your organization's mailing address, as it should appear on invoices.
