Providers are able to transfer DOIs between their Clients. As a Provider, you can only transfer DOIs to a Client you manage. Please [contact us](doc:how-to-contact-datacite) if you want to transfer the DOI to a Client not managed by you. 
[block:api-header]
{
  "title": "Transfer a single DOI"
}
[/block]
Navigate to the record for the DOI you would like to transfer. Click the *Transfer DOI* button in the upper left to begin the process.

[block:api-header]
{
  "title": "Transfer all DOIs from one Client"
}
[/block]
Go to the Clients tab of the Provider dashboard. Select the Client from whom you'd like to transfer DOIs. You are now viewing the Client dashboard, but with special Provider privileges. 

Go to the DOIs tab of the Client dashboard. You will see the list of all DOIs managed by that Client. Click the *Transfer* button in the upper left to begin the transfer of all of that Client's DOIs.

[block:api-header]
{
  "title": "Transfer DOIs to multiple Clients"
}
[/block]
If you want to transfer DOIs to multiple Clients, you can either [transfer each DOI individually](#section-transfer-a-single-doi), or [contact DataCite](doc:how-to-contact-datacite) to manually complete the transfer for you.
