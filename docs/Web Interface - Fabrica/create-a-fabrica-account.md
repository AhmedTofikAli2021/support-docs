To obtain a Fabrica account your organization needs to be a DataCite Member or part of a consortia.

1. If your organization is a part of a consortia please contact your Consortium Lead for account credentials. 

2. If your organization is a Direct Member your credentials should have been provided during your onboarding process. If you do not yet have credentials for Fabrica, please [contact us](mailto:support@datacite.org).

3. If you manage a Repository please contact your Member organization for log in credentials.

The ID is the username and you will need to create a password. The email address associated with the account will receive an automatic email from the Fabrica system prompting them to set a password for the account. 

If you are interested in membership please review the information on our [website](https://datacite.org/become.html). If you are not sure if your organization is a Member, please see the full list [here](https://datacite.org/members.html).
