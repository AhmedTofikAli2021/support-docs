Consortium leads and the Consortium Organizations can update the account settings, which includes contact information and demographic information. These settings are DataCite's primary source for being able to contact the relevant parties at your organization. 

To modify the Consortium Organization settings, go to the Settings tab on the Consortium Organization dashboard. Click the *Update Organization* button in the upper left.

For detailed descriptions of the fields, see [Field Descriptions for Consortium Organizations Settings](doc:field-descriptions-for-consortium)
